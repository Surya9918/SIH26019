# POLICY INNOVATION LAB: SIMULATION SPECIFICATION — SIH26019
## National Digital Platform for Land Governance

---

## 1. Simulation Philosophy
The Policy Innovation Lab allows researchers and policy analysts to simulate counterfactual land governance interventions before formal notification.
Crucially, all simulation results are clearly labeled:
`OFFICIAL MODEL SIMULATION — EXPLORATORY SCENARIO ESTIMATE. NOT A PREDICTIVE GUARANTEE.`

---

## 2. Model Parameters
1. **Urban Growth Velocity** ($r_u$): % annual expansion of built-up footprint (1.0% to 6.0%).
2. **Agricultural Conservation Buffer** ($b_{agri}$): Mandatory green-belt radius around metropolitan boundaries (0.0 to 8.0 km).
3. **Transit-Oriented Density Multiplier** ($m_{tod}$): Floor area ratio / density concentration factor (1.0x to 3.0x).
4. **Forest Protection Mandate**: Binary constraint preventing forest conversion.

---

## 3. Output Indicators
- Land area distribution at 2035 horizon (sq km)
- Prime agricultural land conserved (sq km)
- Carbon sink preservation (MT CO2 equivalent avoided)
- Infrastructure capital expenditure savings (₹ Crores saved by avoiding low-density horizontal sprawl)
